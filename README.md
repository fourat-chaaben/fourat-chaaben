# Fourat Chaaben

Business Informatics student in Munich. I automate what I do not want to do twice by hand.

**[lei-document-validator](https://github.com/fourat-chaaben/lei-document-validator)**
is the clearest example of that. At EQS Group I spent two years checking financial
documents and LEI records by hand, more than 20 a day. Then I rebuilt that check in
Python: a central rule set instead of special cases per field, ISO 17442 checksum
validation, duplicate detection, CSV and JSON reports, and exit codes so a scheduler
can act on the result. Unit-tested and documented.

### What I work with
